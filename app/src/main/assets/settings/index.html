      Voice

      Continuous conversation reads Ara's answers aloud, then opens the mic. A pause as long as the first value ends what you say and sends it; if you say nothing, the mic closes after the second. The same for every endpoint.

      Shelf

      Threads, chats and projects you minimize wait on the shelf at the foot of every page. When it is full, the item used longest ago with nothing new on it makes room; items with an answer or messages waiting are never dropped. The same size for every endpoint.

      Home screen

      Split screen

      The home screen cannot share the screen with an app, so Ara has a second window for split screen. It shows the same page, in the same place, and what you open from it goes in the other half.

      Apps, web apps and links from the home screen open in split screen, next to Ara. Long-press one to open it full screen instead.

      When the split-screen Ara is not among the recent apps, the home screen puts it back there, ready to pair with an app.

      Launcher bar

      Apps, links, web-app pages and folders on the bar at the bottom. A long press on an entry of the bar edits or moves it too.

      Pull the bar up for more apps, the most recently used first (those on the bar are on the bar). It goes up as far as the web app's menu; a tap above it, or opening an app, puts it down again. Long-press an app there to open it the other way, pin it or put it in a folder.

      Keyboard for other devices

      This phone as a Bluetooth keyboard for a device that cannot run Retinue itself — a headset, a TV, someone else's computer. Text you ask Ara to type, and passwords from your password manager, arrive there as key presses. Passwords go through a dialog of the app's own and are never seen by the dashboard or by Ara.

      Updates

      Ara then checks when it comes to the front, at most every six hours, and installs a new build over Wi-Fi once a voice conversation has ended. Installing restarts Ara. Off: nothing is fetched until you check here.

      Android does not let Ara install apps yet. Allow it once; updates then install without asking each time.

      Health data

      Health Connect is not available on this phone, so there is no health data to send.

      Health data from the phone's Health Connect, such as a watch, a ring or a blood-pressure app, goes to this endpoint's health records. Off: nothing is read or sent for it, not even on request.

      Wake phrase

      Say the phrase to open a voice conversation with . Each endpoint has its own: one working in the background still listens for its phrase, and hearing it brings that endpoint to the front. Detection runs on the phone; no audio leaves it until the web app opens the mic.

      Lower wakes more easily, and more often by mistake. 0.5 is the default.

      Phrase model

      The models are kept on the phone, for every endpoint; which of them wakes this endpoint is its own choice.
